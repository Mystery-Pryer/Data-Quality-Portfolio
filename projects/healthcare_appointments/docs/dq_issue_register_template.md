## Severity Guide

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Issue may affect financial, regulatory, patient/customer safety, or executive reporting decisions. |
| High | Issue affects important operational reporting or downstream workflows. |
| Medium | Issue affects analysis quality but has limited immediate operational risk. |
| Low | Issue is mostly cosmetic, minor, or low-volume. |

## Notes

Severity should be based on business impact, affected record count, affected percentage, downstream dependency, and whether the issue can be corrected or prevented.
